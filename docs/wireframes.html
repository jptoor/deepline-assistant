Deepline Assistant wireframes

Every screen a sales rep sees, at real panel width, in the Deepline app's own styles. Each screen has one pinned action: the most important job for that moment. Each frame states the rep's goal, what they must see, that action, and what Deepline does in the background.

Rules every screen follows

One pinned actionThe bottom of the panel always holds the single most important action, with what it will do and what it costs. Everything else is a quiet text link.
Nothing technical for repsNo keys, settings, play names to set up, or URLs. The central team chooses the actions and handoffs once in Deepline; reps just click.
One-step sign-inInstalling opens Deepline sign-in. If the rep is already signed in, the panel is connected with no steps at all.
What you have, then what to doSaved results show before any paid action. Once a result is in, the pinned action becomes the handoff or Copy email, never a second paid lookup.
Credits on the buttonEvery action shows its cost. Anything over 25 credits asks once in the panel. No charge.
Only the address and titleThe panel reads the page address and tab title, nothing else, so LinkedIn sees nothing. Deepline does every lookup.
