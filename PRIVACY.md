# Privacy

Deepline Assistant reads the active tab's URL and title. It does not read page content, run scripts on websites, or expose files to websites.

When you run a play, the extension sends the fields that play declares (for example a LinkedIn profile URL, a company domain, or a CRM record id, plus a name taken from the tab title) to your Deepline workspace at code.deepline.com. Results are stored in your workspace database (`assistant.records`) under your account. Nothing is sent anywhere else, and there is no analytics.

Stored in the browser: the Deepline URL, a device API key if you connected by device approval, and your pinned plays. Disconnect in Settings to remove the key; revoke it in Deepline to invalidate it.
