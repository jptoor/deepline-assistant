# Design context

## Who
Sales reps and SDRs. Not engineers: they will never see an API key, a CLI, a
play name they have to configure, SQL, or a URL setting. A central team
(RevOps / whoever manages Deepline) sets up which actions and handoffs exist,
once, in the Deepline app. Reps install, sign in once, and click.

## Jobs (from docs/research/jtbd-journey-rubric.md)
1. Reach this person: work email and phone for whoever is on screen.
2. Stay where I am: never leave LinkedIn, the CRM, or the site.
3. Hand it off without copy-paste: CRM contact, sequence.
4. Don't pay twice: show what I already have before any paid action.
5. Turn a search into a list, without paging.
6. Trust it before I send: status and source on every value.
Hygiene: don't break my browser, don't make me log in again.

## Rules
- Every screen has exactly ONE priority action, pinned at the bottom of the
  panel, and it says exactly what will happen and what it costs
  ("Find email + phone · ~3 credits"). Everything else is a quiet text button.
- Sign-in is one step: install opens Deepline sign-in; if the rep is already
  signed in, the panel is connected with zero steps.
- Existing data first, then the action. A finished result's priority action is
  the handoff (or "Copy email"), never "run it again".
- Plain sales words: look up, email, phone, add to HubSpot, import, credits.
  Never: play, run, schema, table, key, workspace setup, webhook.

## Visual identity
Deepline app tokens (deepline-api apps/deepline-api/src/app/globals.css):
silver surfaces, monochrome chrome (a button is a shape, not a colour), colour
only for status (success/error/info roles), Geist 14px body / 16px title,
Geist Mono for values, radii 6 (chip) / 10 (control) / 14 (surface), hit
targets 40px+. Instrument Serif is marketing-only; never in the panel.
No gradients, glass, glows, emoji, icon-grid cards, all-caps eyebrows, or
accent stripes.
